How I Think

I approach strategy by starting with the problem and the people affected by it, then connecting insight to practical execution.

01

Start with behaviour

I look at what people already do, say and struggle with before jumping into content, campaigns or tactics.

02

Find the insight

I look for the simple human truth behind the problem — something specific enough to shape a meaningful strategy and natural enough for the audience to recognise.

03

Connect the idea to an objective

Creative work should have a reason to exist. I connect ideas to objectives such as awareness, engagement, acquisition, retention or improving the customer experience.

04

Make the strategy executable

I prefer strategies that can move beyond a presentation and become real content, campaigns, processes and measurable next steps.
